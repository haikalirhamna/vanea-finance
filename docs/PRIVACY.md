# Vanea — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-10-10 · **Developer:** @haikalirhamna · **Contact:** _add a contact email before publishing_

Vanea is a personal finance app that works entirely on your phone.

## What Vanea does with your data

- **Everything stays on your phone.** Your income, costs, salary, debts, investments and notes are stored in a database on your device, encrypted with a random key kept in the Android Keystore.
- **Vanea does not send your data anywhere.** There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting inside the app. The production build does not request the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot connect to the network.
- **Nothing is shared with third parties.**

## What you can choose to do

- **Export a backup.** You can create an encrypted backup file protected by a passphrase you choose. Vanea hands the file to the Android share sheet; where it goes (your files, your cloud storage, another app) is up to you. Vanea cannot recover a forgotten passphrase.
- **Restore a backup.** Vanea reads a backup file you pick and replaces the data on your phone with it, after showing you what it contains.
- **Local reminders.** If you allow notifications, Vanea schedules reminders on your phone (payday, bills, subscriptions, monthly reflection, backup). They are created and shown on the device; no notification server is involved.
- **App lock.** If you turn it on, Vanea asks for your fingerprint, face or screen lock using Android's own prompt. Vanea never sees or stores your biometrics.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | Local reminders you can switch off in Settings |
| Biometrics / screen lock | The optional app lock |
| (No INTERNET, no contacts, no location, no camera, no microphone, no storage access) | |

## Your data and your control

- **Delete everything:** uninstall Vanea, or clear its storage in Android settings. Because the data exists only on your phone, that removes it. Backup files you exported are yours to delete.
- **If you lose your phone or uninstall without a backup, the data cannot be recovered.** Vanea has no copy.
- Android Auto Backup is switched off for Vanea, because it would copy the database without its Keystore key.

## Children

Vanea is not directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with an updated date before the change takes effect.
